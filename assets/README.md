# Sunofriend brand assets

The identity visualises Sunofriend's central transformation: an organic cyan
audio waveform becomes precise coral MIDI blocks while the overall path forms
a friendly `S` silhouette.

## Files

- `brand/sunofriend-logo.png` — square symbol for avatars and small icons.
- `brand/sunofriend-listener-banner-v3.png` — current listener-facing project
  banner with the MIDI plus song-mix message.
- `brand/sunofriend-social-preview-1280x640-v1.png` — crop-safe, sub-1 MB
  GitHub/social preview derived from the current banner.
- `social/out-of-place-interpolation-square-v1.png` — square SoundCloud track
  art and listener-facing feed card.
- `social/out-of-place-interpolation-story-v1.png` — vertical Story/Status
  artwork for the interpolation example.
- `brand/sunofriend-banner-v2.png` — earlier MIDI-focused landscape banner.
- `social/the-aisle-at-lidl-square-v2.png` — earlier worked-example feed post.
- `social/the-aisle-at-lidl-story.png` — earlier worked-example Story/Status.

Core palette: deep midnight navy, electric cyan and warm coral-orange. Keep the
logo clear of other marks and retain generous padding around the S silhouette.

## Generation

The original and v3 raster assets were created with ChatGPT's built-in
image-generation model using structured logo and campaign prompts. The
1280 × 640 social preview is a centred crop and resize of the approved v3
banner; it contains no newly generated content.

The complete structured prompts are preserved in [`PROMPTS.md`](PROMPTS.md).

Prompt set:

1. **Logo:** an original minimal S-shaped audio waveform resolving into a
   piano-roll grid; flat cyan/coral/navy palette; strong small-size silhouette;
   no text or third-party marks.
2. **Banner:** preserve the approved symbol; exact text “SUNOFRIEND”, “FROM AI
   STEMS TO PLAYABLE MIDI” and “MUSIC MAKERS • DAW READY”; wide safe layout.
3. **Square example:** an abstract supermarket aisle made from MIDI lanes;
   exact “THE AISLE AT LIDL” worked-example copy; no retailer branding.
4. **Story example:** recompose the square campaign as a safe-margin 9:16
   layout with “LISTEN TO VERSION 1”.
5. **Listener banner v3:** show sound becoming an editable MIDI arrangement;
   exact “HEAR IT. EDIT IT. MAKE IT YOURS.” and “STEMS → MIDI + SONG MIX”
   copy.
6. **Out of Place square:** make the song title the listener-first focus and
   label it “A SUNOFRIEND INTERPOLATION”.
7. **Out of Place Story:** recompose the interpolation campaign vertically
   with “LISTEN NOW” inside safe margins.

Text corrections were also made with the built-in image model. No local
logo substitution was used. The only mechanical derivative is the documented
1280 × 640 social-preview crop.
