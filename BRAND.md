# Sunofriend brand and name

> **Listen deeper. Create further.**

## Origin story

Every song begins with a simple invitation: **सुनो — suno — listen.**

Sunofriend takes its name from the familiar Hindi imperative **सुनो**
(*suno*), from **सुनना** (*sunnā*), “to hear” or “to listen.” Paired with
“friend,” the name carries two connected readings: **“Listen, friend”** and
**“a friend that listens.”**

That is the role Sunofriend is designed to play. It listens to the separated
parts of a song, offers editable musical interpretations, and hands the music
back to its creator. It does not replace the musician or make the final
musical decision.

**Sunofriend: a friend that listens, so your music can keep growing.**

## Naming rules

- Write the product name as **Sunofriend**, one word.
- Use **Suno Friend** only while explaining the “Listen, friend” wordplay.
- On the first outward-facing reference, pair the name with the strapline or
  origin note.
- When referring to the separate service, write **Suno, the AI music company**
  or **Suno Inc.** on first reference.
- Do not call Sunofriend a Suno product, official companion, integration,
  extension, client, plugin, or partner.
- Do not use Suno’s logo, brand styling, screenshots, or trademark as part of
  the Sunofriend identity.
- It is fine to describe an authorised workflow factually, for example:
  **Suno (third-party) → Moises → Sunofriend → DAW**.

## Product doorway

For a non-technical audience, present the product in this order:

1. **Hear an example.**
2. **Open sunofriend.com and copy the two Codex prompts.**
3. **Choose demo, existing stems or help obtaining authorised stems.**
4. **Listen to the resulting WAV.**
5. **Open the MIDI in a DAW or explore alternatives in Studio.**

Do not lead a newcomer with Git, Python, Homebrew, FluidSynth, model names or
the full command catalogue. Those details remain available to developers and
Studio users, but they are implementation details rather than the promise.

The copy must not imply that an ordinary browser chat can control a Mac.
Agent-led installation requires a coding-agent surface with local file and
terminal access. Setup changes and downloads must remain permission-gated.

## Visual identity

The established Sunofriend identity remains:

- deep midnight navy;
- electric cyan;
- warm coral-orange with violet accents; and
- an organic waveform becoming an `S` and editable MIDI blocks.

Primary outward-facing banners should carry:

1. **SUNOFRIEND**
2. **सुनो / SUNO = LISTEN**
3. **LISTEN DEEPER. CREATE FURTHER.**
4. **NOT AFFILIATED WITH SUNO INC.**

The independence line should be legible but secondary to the product name and
strapline. The standalone symbol remains suitable for small avatars when the
profile bio or nearby copy includes the compact distinction.
